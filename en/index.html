Starting from Intention: Is Language a First-Class Citizen?

Published Aug 27, 2026 ~12 min read

Robot learning, at bottom, is learning a mapping from intention to action. Language is currently the most dominant representation of intention, but stepping back, it may not be the “first-class citizen.” This article begins from the mathematical perspective of multivalued mappings and mutual information, then unfolds along three routes — language hierarchies, world models, and in-context learning — and closes with a suspicion: perhaps all of these intention enhancements are merely transitional solutions for an era of scarce data.
